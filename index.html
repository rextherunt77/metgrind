    Press Start camera. Prop the phone up so your whole body is in view, from the side or front.
    With gestures on, reps only count after a start. Gestures are read only while you stand, held for about a second, and the hands must be clearly visible to the camera. Arms out front during a squat can look like open palms, so the stop gesture is ignored mid-rep.
    A burpee counts when you go down until the torso is near horizontal, come back to standing (and with the jump variant, jump) . Film from the side with the phone fixed and level. The jump is measured against your standing foot height, so don't move the phone mid-set.
    Each lunge (either leg, forward, reverse or walking) is one rep. It needs a front knee bend, the back knee dropped below the front one (this tells it from a squat), and an upright torso. Film from the side.
    A rep needs all three at the bottom: knee angle low enough, hips below the knees, torso upright. 180° knee = straight leg. Camera tilt affects the hip and lean numbers, so keep the phone level.
