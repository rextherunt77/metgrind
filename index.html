Press Start camera. Prop the phone up so your whole body is in view, from the side or front.
With gestures on, squats only count after a start. Gestures are read only while you stand, held for about a second, and the hands must be clearly visible to the camera. Arms out front during a squat can look like open palms, so the stop gesture is ignored mid-squat.
A rep needs all three at the bottom: knee angle low enough, hips below the knees, torso upright. 180° knee = straight leg. Camera tilt affects the hip and lean numbers, so keep the phone level.
